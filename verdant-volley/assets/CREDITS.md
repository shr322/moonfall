# Asset credits

## Nature models

**Kenney Nature Kit 2.1** by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/).

- Official asset page: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- License: **Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)**.
- License terms: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- The original distribution license is included as `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt`.
- Selected trees, bushes, rocks, flowers, mushrooms, and stumps are converted
  from the original OBJ files into script-loadable geometry in
  `kenney-nature.js`. Coordinates and diffuse material colors are preserved;
  polygon faces are triangulated. The game scales and places these models and recolors foliage and bark to a muted forest palette at runtime.
